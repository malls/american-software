AS-82: extract makeWatcher() from main() and put the wiring under test

main() was ~400 lines that nothing executed: the AS-27 heartbeat call in
poll(), the lock take in fire() and release in settle(), the AS-21 spawn
argv/env call site, the highwater write, the AS-100 tick events, the tick
box and shutdown() were all unguarded. Priya's M3b deleted the heartbeat
call and 255 tests stayed green.

makeWatcher({config, paths, logsDir, watchDir, log, pid, isPidAlive, now,
spawnFn, createLog, exit, ...ops}) is the sixth factory beside the five
that already exist. poll/fire/settle/shutdown/readSentinel/pruneTickLogs
move verbatim into its closure under the plan's substitution table; the
ops are built inside start() so the sequence of side effects after the
single-instance check is unchanged. main() is now config, paths, log,
the factory call, start(), and the two signal handlers.

test/watcher-main.test.js drives the factory with a controllable clock,
an injected pid/liveness probe and a fake spawn, over a real temp data
dir (the lock, loop mirror and events stream are bytes on disk, not mock
call lists). test/watcher-process.test.js runs the real entry point once
against a temp root with every fence set, to cover the residue no fake
can hold: the interval actually firing, and SIGTERM exiting 0.

Host: 478 tests, 0 fail (master baseline 468 + 10 new).
