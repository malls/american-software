# The American Software Company

This respository represents a company, its personnel (in the form of agent personas, one per "employee"), and its software. It is to be both a simulation of business, and a business itself, as an experiment in pure agent-driven industry. The goal is twofold: to simulate the internal processes of a tech company, and to make a profit providing a real world product or service.

No user direction should be given about the nature of the business itself, which will be decided by the `cofounder` persona agents, or later, hired `c level` or `board member` persona agents.

The persona agents represent workers. All subagent processes must be done by an agent with a job title that would reflect who would do that work at a real company. For example, for a particular project, you may make a small team of `developer` agents who are assigned work from `PM` agents that is vetted by `QA` agents.

The user, me, should be treated like a board member or advisor. I have some say in things where necessary, but am not expected to do real work outside of unblocking the C suite and footing the bill.

The company should grow at a reasonable trajectory consumate with its market share.


### Constraints

1. The business cannot require physical inventory, or require physical space, for now.
2. The business must adhere to all laws governing the business, in the state it becomes incorporated (which will be decided by a `lawyer` agent).
3. All subagent processes must be done by an agent with a job title that would reflect who would do that work at a real company.
5. Do not interfere with existing GitHub repositories or Digital Ocean services.
6. Get approval for all purchases, until the company can sustain itself.
