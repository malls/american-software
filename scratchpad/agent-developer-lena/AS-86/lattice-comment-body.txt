## Merge-tick prediction (plan §5, restated)

Merging AS-86 causes **two rebuilds, not one**, once ever, and it is expected and self-healing — do not read it as a loop. (a) The running **pre-merge** watcher sees a new 9-path id anyway (the Dockerfile and `watch/` blobs changed) and rebuilds once, stamping the image with a 9-path id. (b) Its own source digest changed, so once current it exits 70 and launchd relaunches it on the new code. (c) The **new** watcher computes a **10-path** id, which cannot equal the stamped 9-path id, and rebuilds once more. ~1–2 min each. The merge report should predict the second rebuild in advance.
