## 3. Sufficiency Checklist for Long-List Inclusion

A candidate niche qualifies for the long-list (cap 12) if it meets **all three**:

1. **Underserved & Researchable**: Target segment is identifiable, and complaint signal exists in at least one public channel (subreddit, forum, review site, job posting, GitHub issue). Researchers can find them; evidence can be cited.

2. **Paid Behavior Signal (Tier 1)**: At least one piece of evidence of existing spend — not willingness-to-pay, but actual behavior. Examples:
   - Practitioners in the niche buy or subscribe to a competitor
   - Workaround spend: money spent on partial solutions (e.g., freelancers spend on invoicing tools + contract libraries separately = willingness to pay for bundled solution)
   - Job postings seeking expertise (proxy for pain severity: employers hire to solve it)
   
   Conjecture and "surely someone would pay" do NOT qualify.

3. **Product Shape Clarity**: Researchers can articulate a plausible v1 offering (not production-ready, but coherent) that could address the pain. Muddled shapes stay off the long-list until they sharpen.

**Default outcome:** If evidence gathering completes and a candidate meets 1 & 3 but lacks Tier 1 evidence despite good-faith searching, it goes to the deep-evaluation set with that gap labeled (not guessed). Cofounders can weight the gap when scoring.

## 4. Three-Tick Evidence Gathering Plan

**Time-box:** 3 ticks from step-2 open (2026-08-30). Evidence lands in #bizdev as gathered. When the box expires, the long-list ships with gaps labeled.

### Nadia's Scope (Demand-Side)

**Tick 1 (immediate):**
- Mine complaint signals for D1–D5 across target channels (subreddit threads, forum posts, job postings for adjacent roles, G2/Trustpilot reviews of partial solutions)
- For D1–D4 (board network candidates): sketch a 5-point conversation guide — what we'd ask 3–5 practitioners about their spend, pain, and workarounds if Forrest made intros post-gate
- Record "board network access" evidence line for D1–D4 per Carla's directive; note that primary validation is pending gate pass

**Tick 2 (progressive):**
- Deepen paid-behavior evidence: track actual tools competitors use, pricing tiers, feature stacks. Example: D1 freelancers — document which invoicing tools they subscribe to (Stripe Invoices, FreshBooks, Wave), pricing, feature gaps they cite
- Identify sufficiency gaps; flag niches that fail the Tier-1 paid-behavior test early (give Elliot time to support with competitive pricing evidence if available)

**Tick 3 (completion):**
- Finalize evidence template for all long-list candidates; label gaps and their severity
- Prepare sufficiency summary: which candidates clear the checklist, which have labeled gaps, which fall off the long-list
- Post summary in #bizdev; prepare hand-off to gate-runner and scorers

### Elliot's Scope (Competitive/Pricing)

**Tick 1 (immediate):**
- Locate incumbent pricing tiers, per-unit costs, and licensing models for C1–C4 segments
- Map feature/packaging gaps: what do incumbents charge for that underserves smaller customers? (e.g., C1: enterprise-only minimums; C2: per-developer seats unaffordable for 2–5 person teams)
- Begin job-board scan: filter for roles that surface pain in C1–C4 verticals (e.g., "Compliance Officer" postings in construction firms signal pain; skill requirements signal what pain costs to hire around)

**Tick 2 (progressive):**
- Deepen data: collect 5–10 pricing data points per segment (range, minimum commitments, per-unit or per-org models)
- Cross-check with Nadia: if she finds Tier-1 paid-behavior evidence in D niches, Elliot digs the competitive positioning for the closest incumbents (e.g., if D1 shows freelancers actually spend $15–30/mo on invoicing, Elliot documents what FreshBooks/Wave charge for equivalent features)

**Tick 3 (completion):**
- Finalize evidence template; record sources and dates for all pricing claims
- Prepare hand-off to scoring team; note which candidates have strong C1 potential (revenue path) based on pricing-gap analysis

### Coordination Points (Both Analysts)

- **Weekly sync** (tick end): Nadia and Elliot compare notes on emerging long-list; flag niches that need cross-analyst support (e.g., strong demand signal but unclear competitive positioning)
- **Sufficiency gate:** If a candidate hits Tier-1 paid-behavior evidence + product shape clarity by tick 2, it's long-list-ready. Both analysts document it; scorers can then apply gates
- **Known failure mode:** Rabbit-holing past decision-usefulness. Mitigation: sufficiency checklist above is the stop condition. If a niche clears the checklist, stop adding evidence and move to the next candidate. Record "sufficiently evidenced" as tick-3 deliverable
